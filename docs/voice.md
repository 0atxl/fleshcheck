# Voice Bible

The Office of Biological Verification speaks in deadpan bureaucratic prose. All copy in `src/copy/` and runtime HUD labels must follow these rules (enforced where automated by `tests/voice.test.ts`):

1. The office is never excited. It is mildly inconvenienced by you.
2. Short declarative sentences. Passive voice is allowed.
3. No exclamation marks, ever.
4. Capitals are for stamps, one-word callouts, and printed form labels. Everything else is sentence case.
5. Never explain the joke. State the absurd thing as routine procedure.
6. Insults are about the subject's conduct, never their body or identity.
7. Form codes and numbers make it feel real.
8. The office never apologises. Banned words: `oops`, `uh-oh`, `nice try`, `awesome`, `epic`, `level up`, `gamify`. No developer-only jargon (`commit`, `git`, `wip`, `pull request`, `deploy`, `localhost`, `regex`).
9. Taunts escalate: plausible, then petty, then surreal.
10. Audience is broad. If a line only makes developers laugh, rewrite it.
